# Sales / Offer Research Checklist

Research:

- ICP
- buying trigger
- urgent before-state
- desired outcome
- substitutes
- proof
- objections
- price anchors
- competitor packaging
- acquisition channel
- sales cycle
- fulfillment cost
- support burden
- recurring revenue
- risk reversal

## Evidence rules

Separate:

- observed evidence
- reasonable inference
- hypothesis to test

Do not call an offer validated because one anecdote performed well.

## Output

End with:

- offer
- buyer
- promise
- proof
- price/packaging
- acquisition path
- objection handling
- smallest commercial test
