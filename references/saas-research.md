# SaaS / Software Research Checklist

## Problem

- ICP
- job-to-be-done
- current workflow
- pain frequency
- pain severity
- current workaround
- switching trigger

## Market

- direct competitors
- indirect substitutes
- pricing
- review complaints
- missing features
- switching reasons
- acquisition channels
- willingness-to-pay evidence

## Product

- activation moment
- retention loop
- onboarding
- roles/permissions
- integrations
- billing
- notifications
- reporting
- support burden

## Engineering

- architecture
- data model
- auth
- tenancy
- observability
- security/privacy
- backups
- rate limits
- deployment
- operational cost

Avoid cloning competitor feature lists. Build around the painful workflow.
