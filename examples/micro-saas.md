# Example — Micro-SaaS

Prompt:

> Build a micro-SaaS for marketplace sellers.

Expected research:

- seller workflow
- current substitutes
- competitor matrix
- review complaints
- willingness-to-pay evidence
- acquisition channels
- activation/retention
- support burden
- architecture/integrations
- pricing

The skill should reject "clone every competitor feature" and identify an evidence-backed wedge.
